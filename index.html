A little about me

A bit more about me.

I'm a content creator from South Africa. What started as me just talking to a camera has turned into a real love for inspiring people and sharing my story with them.

I try hard to stay authentic. I've turned down big offers because the product wasn't something I agreed with, so for me it's honestly never about the price. It's about the brand, and whether I'd actually use it in my own life. My followers trust me, and the engagement is as strong as it is because they know I'd never put something in front of them that I don't believe in.

I'm opinionated, straight to the point, and sometimes a bit too honest. But in a world filling up with AI and fake stories, that's what people actually want to see. And that's what I can give them for you.

  • I do my best work with some creative freedom, but if you have a brief, I'll stick to it properly.
  • Quick, open communication. I'd rather over-explain than leave you wondering where things are.
  • Song promotions, event attendance, photoshoots, product reviews, whatever you're after, I can deliver.
